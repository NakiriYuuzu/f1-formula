#           fake DOM of the render tests; three's own Frustum decides what is submitted. Chase camera 7 m behind and 2.2 m above car 10
#           (fov 68, 16:9). Pack scenarios sample the bench's timed window (0.75 s to 5.75 s after the 28 s run-up). Sky, rain,
#           particles, shadow and post passes are not counted, so only the low tier is comparable with the bench.

#           in the rain than the bench's autopilot player does, so its pose differs. Draw calls overshoot by 5-39 %. Use the figures to
#           rank cuts and to see the shares, not as exact counters.
# reading:  nothing here is a time. It says what changes in the counters; whether the GPU or the CPU of a weak machine is bound by
#           triangles or by draw calls is not measured by it (on the M2 Pro the low-tier ms does not follow the triangle count:
#           grid-monza 522k = 2.9 ms, pack-spa 329k = 3.8 ms).

# result:   (1) The triangles of a low-tier frame are not only the scenery. At the standing grid in Monza: scenery ~278k (55 %), pit crews
#               140k (28 %), cars 89k (17 %). The pit crews are rigid 840-triangle figures at low (96k suit + 38k gear for 160 people,
#               10 draw calls), heavier than the articulated rig of the medium tier (90k): low pays more for the crews than medium.
#               They are drawn whenever the pit lane is in view (also night-singapore: 126k of 408k).
#           (2) Splitting the merged scenery meshes into cells so that three can cull them (cull-probe): at a 1000 m cell the mean
#               submitted triangles fall by 74k (monza), 80k (spa) and 72k (singapore) while the submitted draw calls double
#               (51 -> 102, 54 -> 115, 38 -> 106). That is 1.0-1.5k triangles saved per draw call added, and smaller cells are worse
#               (350 m: -109k triangles, +227 calls). Not a win under the rule that a cut must not add more draw calls than it
#               pays for; dropped. The track-length ribbons (steel, concrete, paint, kerbs, runoff, fence) are in view along the whole
#               straight, so cells cull little of them.
#           (3) Car LOD switch distances (lod=27,60 against 35,110): -3 to -5 % triangles and -12 to -19 % draw calls in the frame
#               (grid-monza 193 -> 162 calls, -25k triangles). The followed-car test limits LOD1 to >= 27 m.
